# EV Ride Operations Analytics and Business Insights – Power BI Dashboard

## Project Overview

Developed an interactive **Power BI dashboard** to analyze the operational and business performance of an **EV ride-hailing platform**. The dashboard integrates ride, fleet, charging, and demand data to provide a comprehensive view of operational efficiency and customer demand.

## Key Analysis

* Analyzed **ride volume and demand trends** across different time periods.
* Evaluated **EV fleet utilization and vehicle availability**.
* Analyzed **charging activity and charging efficiency**.
* Identified **peak demand periods and operational patterns**.
* Examined key metrics related to **customer experience and ride operations**.
* Used interactive filters and visualizations to enable **data-driven exploration**.

## Dashboard Features

* Interactive KPI cards for key operational metrics.
* Ride and demand trend analysis.
* Fleet utilization and availability analysis.
* Charging performance analysis.
* Time-based and categorical filtering.
* Interactive charts and business performance visualizations.
* Data-driven insights and recommendations for operational improvement.

## Business Insights

The dashboard helps identify operational inefficiencies and demand patterns, providing actionable recommendations to improve **fleet utilization, charging efficiency, ride availability, and customer experience**.
